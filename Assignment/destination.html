The Pink City — Jaipur

History Culture Tourism

History

Jaipur was founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, a visionary leader who planned the city with precision and symmetry.

Culture

The culture of Jaipur blends tradition and royalty. Its music, art, and festivals represent the true spirit of Rajasthan.

Tourism

Famous for Amer Fort, City Palace, and Hawa Mahal, Jaipur attracts millions of tourists every year.
